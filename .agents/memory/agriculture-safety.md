---
name: Agriculture safety language
description: Durable product language rules for crop health predictions and recommendations.
---

AI image analysis is preliminary visual guidance. It must use “possible” language and a confidence score, never claim that a phone photo measured nutrient percentages, and should direct farmers to laboratory testing for confirmation. Fertilizer and pesticide guidance must defer to product labels, local regulations, and qualified agricultural experts.

**Why:** Crop and nutrient advice can cause real crop or financial harm when a visual estimate is presented as a verified measurement.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in scan results, reports, assistant responses, alerts, and any future AI/backend integration.
