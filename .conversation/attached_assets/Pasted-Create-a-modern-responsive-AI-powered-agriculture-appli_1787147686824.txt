# 3. AI CROP DISEASE DETECTION

Create a feature called:

**“AI Crop Disease Scanner”**

Workflow:

Farmer opens camera/upload image
↓
Upload crop/leaf image
↓
Image quality check
↓
AI identifies crop
↓
AI analyzes symptoms
↓
AI identifies possible disease
↓
Calculate confidence score
↓
Display disease report

Show:

* Crop name
* Possible disease
* AI confidence score
* Symptoms
* Possible causes
* Treatment information
* Prevention
* Recommended next steps

Example:

**Crop:** Cotton

**Possible Disease:** Leaf Spot

**AI Confidence:** 89%

**Symptoms:**

* Brown spots
* Yellowing around affected areas
* Leaf damage

**Possible Cause:**
Fungal infection

**Recommended Action:**
Follow locally approved disease-management guidance and consult an agricultural expert if symptoms continue.

If the image is unclear, do not provide a confident diagnosis.

Display:

**“Please upload a clearer photo of the affected leaf.”**

# 4. AI NUTRIENT DEFICIENCY DETECTION FROM IMAGE

Create a separate feature:

**“AI Nutrient Scan”**

Workflow:

Farmer takes/uploads leaf image
↓
AI checks image quality
↓
AI analyzes leaf color, patterns, spots, yellowing, curling and growth symptoms
↓
AI identifies possible nutrient deficiency
↓
Display ranked possible deficiencies
↓
Give confidence score
↓
Provide recommended next steps

Analyze possible visual symptoms related to:

* Nitrogen (N)
* Phosphorus (P)
* Potassium (K)
* Sulfur (S)
* Magnesium (Mg)
* Calcium (Ca)
* Iron (Fe)
* Zinc (Zn)
* Boron (B)
* Manganese (Mn)
* Copper (Cu)

Example:

**Possible Nutrient Deficiency**

Nitrogen — 82% AI confidence

Possible symptoms:

* Yellowing of older leaves
* Pale green appearance
* Reduced growth

Other possible deficiencies:

1. Nitrogen — 82%
2. Magnesium — 64%
3. Potassium — 41%

IMPORTANT:

The app must NOT claim that a phone camera has directly measured nutrient percentages.

Do NOT show:

“Nitrogen = 37% detected from photo”

Instead show:

**“Possible Nitrogen Deficiency – AI Confidence 82%.”**

Explain that image analysis provides a preliminary visual indication and actual nutrient levels should be confirmed through laboratory soil or plant-tissue testing.

# 12. SAFETY & ACCURACY

The application must clearly distinguish:

### AI Prediction

Visual prediction from crop image.

### Laboratory Measurement

Actual soil/plant laboratory result.

### Agricultural Recommendation

General guidance based on crop, symptoms, soil and weather.

Never present an AI prediction as a laboratory measurement.

Never invent nutrient percentages.

Never provide unsafe or unsupported pesticide recommendations.

For pesticide/fertilizer information, instruct farmers to follow the product label and local agricultural regulations and consult a qualified agricultural expert where appropriate.
